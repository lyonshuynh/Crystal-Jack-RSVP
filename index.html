Getting There

Fly into Denver International Airport (DEN) — the closest major airport. From DEN, it's roughly a 2-hour drive to Leadville (~120 miles via I-70 W and US-24 S).

Rental car: A high-clearance vehicle is recommended, and AWD or 4WD is ideal for the last few miles of Forest Service Road 110, which is a rough dirt road.

If you don't have the right vehicle or want to carpool, email LyonsHuynh@gmail.com and we'll help coordinate rides.

Trailhead

North Mt. Elbert Trailhead via County Road 11 (Halfmoon Creek Road) near Leadville, CO
Use Forest Service Road 110, Leadville, CO 80461 for GPS

Lodging

The Lodge — We've reserved a large cabin near the trailhead for Wed Sep 2 through Sat Sep 5, open to all on a first-come, first-served basis. Email LyonsHuynh@gmail.com to claim a spot.

On Your Own — Leadville has a range of hotels, vacation rentals, and B&Bs nearby.

Camping — For the bold: dispersed camping near the trailhead is always an option.

What to Wear

Dress for the hike — comfortable layers, broken-in boots, whatever gets you to 14,440 ft. No need to pack dress clothes. The whole idea is to hike, exchange rings somewhere breathtaking, and head back down. The dinner on Sunday is where we'll take the nice photos.

Recommended Gear
  • Headlamp with fresh batteries — essential for the 4:00 AM start
  • Layered clothing — moisture-wicking base, insulating mid-layer, wind and rain shell
  • Warm gloves and a hat — summit temps can drop near freezing
  • Sturdy hiking boots with ankle support
  • Trekking poles — highly recommended, especially on the descent
  • 3+ liters of water — dehydration is faster at altitude
  • High-energy snacks — bars, trail mix, anything portable
  • Sunscreen SPF 50+ and sunglasses — UV is intense above treeline
  • Personal medications and a small first aid kit
  • Fully charged phone with emergency contacts saved
Rain Check

If weather calls for it, the hike moves to Saturday, September 5. The dinner on Sunday, September 6 is unaffected regardless of which day we summit — no action needed on your part.

A Note on Fitness

Mount Elbert is a strenuous hike — approximately 9 miles round trip with 4,700 ft of elevation gain at high altitude. Participants should be in good cardiovascular health. We strongly recommend spending a day or two acclimatizing in Colorado before the hike. This climb is not suitable for those with heart, lung, or significant mobility conditions. Please reach out at LyonsHuynh@gmail.com or 952-688-2363 with any questions about what to expect.

Bringing Kids

Children are welcome at Sunday's dinner — bring the whole family.

For the hike, it's at parents' discretion, but given the 4:00 AM start, 4,700 ft of elevation gain, and altitude near 14,440 ft, we don't recommend it for young kids. If you're considering it, please reach out so we can talk it through.
